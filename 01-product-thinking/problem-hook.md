## Responses

- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** StreamLine Spotlight
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing for 12 months, the company will become known as a “warehouse” of mid-tier content. Subscribers who value quality will keep churning to competitors that feel more curated, engagement will keep falling, and we’ll be forced to compete on price and library size, a race we can’t win against bigger platforms.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** The user is currently forced to scroll endlessly without finding anything worth watching, then leave the app to Google “best movies on StreamLine,” check Reddit or Rotten Tomatoes, or ask friends for recommendations. Often they give up and open a competitor instead. Every time this happens, they learn that StreamLine isn’t where good content lives, which puts their recurring subscription at risk.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must solve rising subscriber churn and declining perceived value by addressing users’ frustration at being unable to find quality content in a 15,000-title library where everything looks the same.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For viewers who value quality over quantity, we will bring the best titles in our library forward, matched to each viewer’s taste, through editorial collections, spotlighted picks, and personalized recommendations, so StreamLine becomes the place they trust to find something great rather than a catalog they scroll past. This is urgent because every month we’re seen as a warehouse, more quality-seeking subscribers build the habit of watching elsewhere, and they’re far harder and more expensive to win back once they’ve left.
